# 📘 Course Overview – Vision-Systems.AI 2026

Welcome to **Vision-Systems.AI**, a comprehensive Computer Vision and AI course designed to bridge the gap between theory, research, and real-world applications.

---

## 🎯 Course Objectives

This course equips students with the theoretical foundations and practical skills needed to:

- Process and analyze digital images and video streams
- Build classical and deep learning-based vision pipelines
- Understand 3D geometry and multi-view vision
- Train, fine-tune, and deploy modern vision models
- Work with foundation models (SAM, CLIP, DINO, etc.)
- Deploy vision systems at scale using cloud and edge platforms
