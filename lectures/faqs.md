# ❓ FAQs – Vision-Systems.AI 2026

Frequently asked questions about the course, field, and resources.

---

## 🎓 About the Course

**Q: What are the prerequisites for this course?**  
A: Basic Python programming and familiarity with linear algebra and calculus. No prior computer vision experience required.

**Q: Is this course free?**  
A: Yes — all materials, lecture notes, and Colab labs are freely accessible.

**Q: How many hours per week should I expect to spend?**  
A: Approximately 8–10 hours per week, including lectures (3h), lab (2h), self-study (3h), and assignment work (2h).

**Q: Will there be a certificate?**  
A: Completion certificates will be awarded to students who finish all assignments and the final project.

---

## 🖥️ Technical Questions

**Q: Can I use Google Colab instead of a local setup?**  
A: Absolutely. Most labs are provided as Colab notebooks. Local setup is recommended for the deployment module.

**Q: What GPU do I need?**  
A: A GPU is not required for most labs. For deep learning modules, Colab's free GPU (T4) is sufficient.

**Q: My OpenCV installation fails — what should I do?**  
A: Try `pip install opencv-python-headless` instead of `opencv-python`. See [installation guide](installation.md) for more.

**Q: Can I use TensorFlow instead of PyTorch?**  
A: PyTorch is the primary framework. TensorFlow examples will be provided where relevant, but assignments use PyTorch.

## 📂 Assignments & Submissions

**Q: Where do I submit assignments?**  
A: Submit via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/purus15987/LearnCV.ai/issues) as directed in each task's instructions.

**Q: Can I work in groups on assignments?**  
A: Assignments are individual. Projects can be done in groups of 2–3.

**Q: What happens if I miss a deadline?**  
A: Contact the instructor before the deadline. Late submissions may be accepted at a 20% penalty.

## 🌐 Career & Field Questions

**Q: What jobs can I get with computer vision skills?**  
A: Computer Vision Engineer, ML Engineer, Research Scientist, Robotics Engineer, AR/VR Developer, Medical Imaging Analyst, and more.

**Q: Should I focus on PyTorch or TensorFlow for jobs?**  
A: PyTorch dominates research and is increasingly preferred in industry. TensorFlow/Keras is common in production pipelines.

**Q: What are the top companies hiring for computer vision roles?**  
A: Google DeepMind, Meta AI, Apple, Tesla, NVIDIA, Waymo, Amazon, Microsoft Research, and numerous startups.

**Q: How do I stay up-to-date with the field?**  
A: Follow [Papers With Code](https://paperswithcode.com/), [arXiv cs.CV](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CV/recent), [@karpathy](https://twitter.com/karpathy), and top CV conference proceedings (CVPR, ECCV, ICCV, NeurIPS).
