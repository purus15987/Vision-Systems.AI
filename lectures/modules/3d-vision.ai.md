# 📐 Module 3: 3D-Vision.AI – Geometry & Three-Dimensional Vision

> **"The world is 3D — our cameras capture its 2D shadow."**

---

## 📋 Module Overview

This module covers the mathematical foundations of 3D vision — from camera models and calibration to stereo reconstruction and structure from motion.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand pinhole camera model and projective geometry
- Perform camera calibration and lens distortion correction
- Implement stereo vision and compute disparity maps
- Reconstruct 3D scenes using Structure from Motion (SfM)
- Work with point clouds and depth sensors

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### Week 8
- Pinhole camera model; intrinsic and extrinsic parameters
- Lens distortion models
- Camera calibration with OpenCV (chessboard method)
- Projective geometry, homogeneous coordinates

### Week 9
- Epipolar geometry; Fundamental and Essential matrices
- Stereo rectification; disparity computation
- Depth estimation from stereo pairs
- Semi-Global Matching (SGM)

### Week 10
- Structure from Motion (SfM) pipeline
- Bundle adjustment
- Point cloud generation and visualization
- Introduction to NeRF (Neural Radiance Fields)

---

## 🧰 Tools

- **OpenCV** – calibration, stereo, SfM
- **Open3D** – point cloud visualization
- **COLMAP** – SfM reconstruction
