# ✨ Module 7: Gen-Vision.AI – Generative Models & Vision Foundation Models

> **"Generative AI doesn't just see the world — it imagines new ones."**

---

## 📋 Module Overview

This module explores the frontier of generative vision: GANs, diffusion models, and the new generation of vision foundation models like SAM, CLIP, and DINO.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand and train Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)
- Work with diffusion models for image synthesis
- Use SAM for zero-shot segmentation
- Apply CLIP for zero-shot image classification
- Fine-tune foundation models for custom tasks

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### Week 19
- GAN fundamentals: generator, discriminator, minimax loss
- Training stability: WGAN, Spectral Normalization
- StyleGAN2 architecture and latent space exploration
- Conditional GANs (cGAN, Pix2Pix, CycleGAN)

### Week 20
- Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM)
- Latent Diffusion Models (Stable Diffusion)
- ControlNet and image-conditioned generation
- Practical: Image inpainting and style transfer

### Week 21
- Segment Anything Model (SAM) — architecture and usage
- CLIP — contrastive image-text pretraining
- DINO / DINOv2 — self-supervised visual features
- Fine-tuning foundation models for domain-specific tasks

---

## 🧰 Tools

- **PyTorch** – GAN and diffusion model training
- **Diffusers (Hugging Face)** – Stable Diffusion pipelines
- **Segment Anything (Meta AI)** – SAM inference
- **CLIP (OpenAI)** – zero-shot classification
- **Transformers (Hugging Face)** – DINOv2
