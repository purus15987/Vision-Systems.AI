# 🎬 Module 4: Temporal-Vision.AI – Video Analysis & Motion Understanding

> **"Motion is the language of the visual world."**

---

## 📋 Module Overview

This module covers the analysis of video streams — from optical flow and background subtraction to multi-object tracking and action recognition.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Compute optical flow to understand pixel motion
- Perform background subtraction for moving object detection
- Implement multi-object tracking pipelines
- Recognize human actions from video sequences

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### Week 11
- Video I/O with OpenCV; frame processing pipelines
- Sparse optical flow: Lucas-Kanade
- Dense optical flow: Farneback, RAFT
- Visualizing flow fields

### Week 12
- Background subtraction: MOG2, KNN, GMG
- Frame differencing
- Shadow removal
- Practical: Security camera motion detection

### Week 13
- Object tracking algorithms: CSRT, KCF, MIL
- Multi-object tracking: SORT, DeepSORT, ByteTrack
- Re-identification (ReID) concepts
- Practical: Traffic tracking system

---

## 🧰 Tools

- **OpenCV** – video I/O, optical flow, background subtraction, trackers
- **PyTorch** – RAFT, action recognition models
- **Supervision** – annotation and tracking utilities
